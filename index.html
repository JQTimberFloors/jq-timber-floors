Award-Winning Project

HIA SA Home of the Year, 2023.

A rare project to work on — an engineered floating loft structure, fitted with 19mm Spotted Gum hardwood flooring and matching steps, sanded and polished to the standard that earned this build South Australia's top home award.

StructureEngineered floating loft
Timber19mm Spotted Gum hardwood
InstallationCustom laid square edge flooring and matching steps
FinishSanded and polished, Bona water-based coating system
RecognitionHIA SA Home of the Year, 2023

Working on an engineered floating loft meant this couldn't be treated as a standard install — the flooring and matching steps were both secret-nailed through the tongue and fully glued to the substrate, giving the 19mm solid Spotted Gum the rigidity to handle the structural movement and load of a full living space above. Working against gravity to hold a solid hardwood floor of this weight firm through nailing and adhesive cure, while still delivering the tight, seamless finish the build demanded, made it one of the more technically demanding jobs we've taken on.

Finished with Bona's water-based coating system to bring out the natural colour variation in the Spotted Gum without the amber tint solvent-based finishes leave behind.

Who's Behind It

Local, family-run, and still doing the sanding ourselves.

JQ Timber Floors has been supplying, installing, sanding and polishing timber floors across the Fleurieu Peninsula since 2005 — from beach shacks to foreshore mansions, and everything in between. What We Do

Every stage of the job, handled by the same crew from start to finish.

From a tired floor that needs new life to a full new install, we handle every stage ourselves — so the finish is consistent from the first sand to the final coat.

01

Floor Sanding

Dust free sanding that strips back years of wear, old finishes and damage to bare, even timber — the foundation for everything that follows.

02

Polishing & Coating

We coat with Bona's water-based systems — low-odour, fast-curing and built to stay clear rather than yellow with age — finished in the sheen that suits the room, from satin living areas to hard-wearing hallways.

03

Restoration & Repairs

Water damage, white ant damage, scratches and board replacement — plus gap filling and insurance work. Bringing older Fleurieu homes' original timber back to a usable, beautiful standard.

04

New Installation

Supply and lay of new timber flooring for renovations, extensions and new builds, finished on site to match the rest of the home.
